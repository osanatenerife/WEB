Frequently asked questions
How many sessions do I need?
It depends on the area and skin type, but on average 8 to 12 sessions are recommended for permanent hair reduction. We'll give you a personalised estimate at your free first consultation.
Does it hurt?
No. Our SHR laser is painless thanks to its progressive sweeping technology, unlike traditional laser. Most clients only feel a mild warmth.
Can I do it in summer or with tanned skin?
Yes. That's one of the advantages of SHR technology over other lasers: it's safe on tanned skin and can be done all year round.
How often should I repeat sessions?
For facial areas we recommend 4 weeks between sessions; for the rest of the body, 6 to 8 weeks, respecting the hair growth cycle to maximise results.
How is SHR different from diode laser?
SHR works with continuous pulses of lower intensity but greater depth, acting more progressively on the follicle and achieving better results in fewer sessions. This makes it hurt much less than traditional diode laser (which emits high-intensity single flashes) and makes it safe on tanned skin, which diode doesn't always allow.
What should I know before my session?
Avoid sun exposure 2 days before and 2 days after each session, and shave the area the day before (not the same day) to prevent cuts or irritation. If you arrive with irritated skin, we may need to postpone the session.

Frequently asked questions
When is wood therapy recommended?
It's recommended if you're looking to shape your body, reduce cellulite, improve circulation or fluid retention, or complement a weight-loss or toning routine. To see results we recommend a minimum of several sessions — available as a package of 3 (210 €) or 6 sessions (360 €).
What is the "Manuela Shala" lymphatic drainage method?
A manual lymphatic drainage technique created by Manuela Shala, internationally recognised as one of the most innovative body-remodelling techniques. It combines deep drainage with remodelling manoeuvres, so it doesn't just de-bloat: it also helps define your silhouette from the first session.
How is it different from therapeutic lymphatic drainage, and who is each one recommended for?
The therapeutic version is gentler and more gradual, designed for conditions or circulatory issues that need a prior assessment. The "Manuela Shala" method is more intense and draining, ideal if you want visible aesthetic results and general wellbeing.
What's the difference between Kobido facial massage and facial lifting?
Kobido is a traditional Japanese facial massage, with rhythmic movements and pressure on specific points that stimulate circulation, drainage and facial muscle tone — very relaxing. Facial lifting focuses on manual traction and firming movements to tighten the skin and define the facial contour — more toning.
