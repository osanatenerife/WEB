Frequently asked questions
How many sessions do I need?
It depends on the area and skin type, but on average 8 to 12 sessions are recommended for permanent hair reduction. We'll give you a personalised estimate at your free first consultation.
Does it hurt?
No. Our SHR laser is painless thanks to its progressive sweeping technology, unlike traditional laser. Most clients only feel a mild warmth.
Can I do it in summer or with tanned skin?
Yes. That's one of the advantages of SHR technology over other lasers: it's safe on tanned skin and can be done all year round.
How often should I repeat sessions?
For facial areas we recommend 4 weeks between sessions; for the rest of the body, 6 to 8 weeks, respecting the hair growth cycle to maximise results.
How is SHR different from diode laser?
SHR works with continuous pulses of lower intensity but greater depth, acting more progressively on the follicle and achieving better results in fewer sessions. This makes it hurt much less than traditional diode laser (which emits high-intensity single flashes) and makes it safe on tanned skin, which diode doesn't always allow.
What should I know before my session?
Avoid sun exposure 2 days before and 2 days after each session, and shave the area the day before (not the same day) to prevent cuts or irritation. If you arrive with irritated skin, we may need to postpone the session.
