Before you book

Booking terms

By booking an appointment at Osana you accept these terms. We summarise them here clearly so you know exactly what you're agreeing to, and what we commit to as well.

1. Booking and payment

To book an appointment, depending on the treatment, you either pay a deposit online (usually 30% of the price, with the rest paid at the center) or the full amount. The exact amount and payment method are always shown before you confirm the booking.

2. Cancellations and rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule your appointment at no cost up to 48 hours in advance from My Bookings. If you cancel or don't show up with less than 48h notice, the amount already paid (deposit or full) is non-refundable.

3. Session packages

If you book a multi-session package, only the first session is scheduled at the time of purchase — the rest are scheduled at the center. The first time you miss an appointment without notice or cancel with less than 48h notice, we'll warn you with no penalty. From the second time onward, one session will be deducted from your package.

4. Gift vouchers

Gift vouchers are valid for 6 months from the purchase date and can be redeemed with a prior booking. To redeem it, the recipient just needs to mention it when booking their appointment.

5. Loyalty programme

When you buy any treatment or session package, you automatically earn a balance on the amount paid: 4% in general, and 6% on the full amount if the payment (or part of it) is made in cash. You don't need to do anything — it's calculated automatically, and we'll email you to let you know your balance.

You can use that balance as a discount on any future single treatment or session package (minimum redemption 10 €, no maximum). It cannot be redeemed towards buying products or gift vouchers. Balance earned during the year expires every December 31st; accumulation starts from zero again on January 1st.

6. Your data

The details you give us when booking (name, phone, email and, if you wish, date of birth) are used only to manage your appointment, package or loyalty balance, and to send you the confirmation, appointment reminder and, if you'd like, occasional communications from Osana. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to osanatenerife@gmail.com.

Any questions about these terms? Message us on WhatsApp before booking.
